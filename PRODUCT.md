# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Residents of Vadodara, Gujarat: people who already ask their society WhatsApp groups, neighbours, and relatives for a trusted electrician, tiffin, tutor, café, or tailor. Bilingual (Gujarati + English, often Gujlish). They arrive on a phone, curious, slightly skeptical of "another app". Secondary: early contributors who want to share people worth knowing, and locals who want to join the waitlist.

## Product Purpose

Halo (spoken "ae halo!") is Vadodara's own hyperlocal companion: ask what the city already knows and get recommendations carried by real local trust, not paid listings. The landing experience exists to make people feel the city is alive and remembered, to collect trusted-local recommendations (parchis), and to grow the early waitlist. Success: a visitor feels "this is ours", shares one recommendation, or joins.

## Brand Personality

Homegrown luxury: rooted, warm, lived-in, quietly confident. Voice is a neighbour who knows everyone, not a platform. Gujlish and Gujarati used with restraint and affection. Three words: warm, remembered, unhurried. Emotional goal: belonging; "the city already knows."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing template: hero + feature cards + testimonials + CTA.
- AI-web aesthetics: purple/blue gradients, floating blobs, bento grids, icon-in-circle features, 3-column rhythm.
- Cream-paper + display-serif "editorial restraint" default (the previous version's lane).
- Noisy directory / contact-finder framing; anything that smells like paid listings.
- Folk-decoration cliché (bandhani borders everywhere, rangoli clipart).

## Design Principles

1. The city unfolds as you scroll: one continuous day (dawn to night), not stacked sections.
2. Trust before promotion: every artifact (chit, stamp, vouch) shows a human carrying the recommendation.
3. Hyperlocal in the material, not just the copy: component names, clock stamps, neighbourhood boards, Gujarati numerals.
4. Motion is narrative: scrubs and reveals advance the story; nothing decorates for its own sake.
5. Restraint with Gujarati: a few words placed with love beat a themed costume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Full prefers-reduced-motion alternatives (no scroll-jacking, content visible). Body contrast >= 4.5:1 on every scene background. Semantic landmarks, labelled inputs, keyboard-reachable intro skip, lang="gu" on Gujarati text.
