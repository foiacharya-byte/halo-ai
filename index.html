Five small stitches, the same way trust has always travelled here.

૧

Someone asks

"Good electrician in Gotri?" "Pure Jain dabba near Manjalpur?" These questions already happen every day.

૨

You remember someone good

A person, shop, café, tutor, home cook, repair guy, local maker, or business that actually helped you.

૩

Name, area, contact if you have it, and why you trusted them. Less than a minute.

૪

We look for consent, context, duplicates, and obvious spam before anything becomes useful to others.
